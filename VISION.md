# Launch Auth — Vision

Launch Auth is an AI Launch & Authority Engine for startups, indie developers, SaaS companies, apps, and small businesses.

Given a company or product URL, Launch Auth should understand the business, identify whether there is a credible launch or authority opportunity, create an evidence-backed campaign, route fulfillment through interchangeable providers and directory adapters, and report observed results.

## Product principle

We sell launch and authority infrastructure—not a number of press-release placements. Distribution is one fulfillment rail underneath the product.

## Boundaries

- Never invent factual claims.
- Never represent submitted or distributed as published.
- Do not promise rankings, backlinks, traffic, or AI visibility.
- Do not bypass CAPTCHAs or anti-bot controls.
- Do not purchase services or commit to providers without explicit approval.

## Status vocabulary

Every important statement is labeled **VERIFIED**, **INFERRED**, **ASSUMED**, or **UNKNOWN** until evidence changes its status.
