# Launch Auth — Decision Log

## 2026-08-26 — Codename

**Decision:** Use Launch Auth as the working codename; branding is intentionally deferred.

**Reason:** The product thesis is broader than press release distribution, and premature naming work would distract from validation.

## 2026-08-26 — Evidence-first fulfillment

**Decision:** Model submitted, distributed, published, indexed, and AI-detected as distinct states.

**Reason:** Customer trust depends on observed outcomes rather than supplier marketing language.

## 2026-08-26 — Interchangeable providers

**Decision:** Use provider adapters behind a stable fulfillment contract.

**Reason:** Supplier terms, pricing, coverage, and reliability are still unknown.

## 2026-08-26 — Directory fulfillment starts assisted

**Decision:** Treat BetaList as EDITORIAL and SourceForge as MANUAL/API-ASSISTED in the first directory adapter registry.

**Reason:** Both require account-level actions and/or editorial or eligibility review. Automation must stop at the human boundary.
